# Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate overall business performance, product category contributions, outlet performance, and sales distribution.

The analysis was performed on a dataset containing **8,523 records**, with the objective of identifying meaningful business insights and providing data-driven recommendations.

## Objectives & Analysis

### 1. Overall Sales Performance
- Analyzed total sales revenue and average sales value.
- Evaluated average product visibility across the supermarket.

### 2. Product Category & Sales Comparison
- Compared sales contributions across product categories.
- Identified the highest- and lowest-performing categories.
- Evaluated sales performance across different outlet types.

### 3. Outlet Performance & Sales Relationships
- Compared Low Fat and Regular product sales across outlets.
- Examined the relationship between product visibility and sales.
- Analyzed product category sales across outlet types.

### 4. Statistical Analysis of Sales
- Calculated average, median, minimum, and maximum sales.
- Evaluated sales variation using standard deviation, variance, and range.
- Identified potential outliers using the IQR method.
- Compared average sales across product categories, outlet types, and outlet locations.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Statistical Functions
- Charts and Data Visualization
- Business Insights and Recommendations

## Key Findings
- Total sales revenue reached approximately **₹12.02 lakh** across 8,523 records.
- The top four product categories contributed approximately **50.60%** of total sales.
- Supermarket Type 1 contributed approximately **65.5%** of total revenue.
- Low Fat products generated higher sales than Regular products.
- No potential sales outliers were identified using the IQR method.
- Product visibility showed no clear relationship with sales.
